## 💡 About the Project

Imagine visiting a hospital, railway station, college, bank, or government office and being unable to communicate your needs because the person assisting you does not understand sign language.

For millions of deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals, this is an everyday challenge.

**SignBridge 360** aims to make essential public services more inclusive by bringing together AI-assisted communication, sign-language resources, text, speech, visual guidance, and human interpreter support in one accessible platform.

Rather than focusing only on sign-language translation, SignBridge 360 addresses the complete communication journey — from asking a simple question to handling an emergency or completing an essential public-service process.

## 🎯 The Problem We Solve

Communication barriers can make everyday activities unnecessarily difficult for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals.

- Difficulty communicating with staff who do not know sign language.
- Limited access to interpreters in essential public places.
- Challenges explaining emergencies quickly.
- Dependence on written communication, which may not always be convenient.
- Lack of accessible instructions for complex procedures.
- Communication difficulties when internet connectivity is unavailable.

**Our vision:** A world where accessibility is not an additional service but a fundamental part of every public interaction.

## ✨ Core Features

SignBridge 360 brings together 10 essential accessibility features.

| # | Feature | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Two-Way Communication | Enables text-to-speech communication and displays responses through text and sign-language resources. |
| 02 | Situation-Based Assistance | Provides contextual communication support for hospitals, railway stations, colleges, banks, and government offices. |
| 03 | Emergency Express | Offers one-tap emergency messages for communicating urgent needs quickly. |
| 04 | Limited AI Sign Recognition | Explores camera-based recognition of a selected, validated vocabulary of signs. |
| 05 | QR Communication Pass | Allows users to carry their communication preferences between supported kiosks. |
| 06 | Human Interpreter Support | Provides a pathway to request qualified human interpreter assistance. |
| 07 | Visual Guidance | Uses icons, illustrations, and step-by-step instructions to simplify public-service procedures. |
| 08 | Queue & Appointment Assistance | Displays tokens, waiting updates, and directions through visual notifications. |
| 09 | Offline Mode | Makes essential communication phrases and sign-language resources accessible without an internet connection. |
| 10 | Accessibility Dashboard | Helps institutions understand common communication barriers through aggregated, privacy-conscious insights. |

## 🚀 What Makes SignBridge 360 Different?

Most basic accessibility tools focus on a single communication method.

SignBridge 360 takes a broader approach by combining:

- **AI-assisted accessibility:** Technology-supported communication and selected sign recognition.
- **Multimodal interaction:** Text, speech, sign-language resources, and visual instructions.
- **Context-aware support:** Communication assistance tailored to different public-service environments.
- **Accessibility beyond connectivity:** Essential resources designed to remain available offline.
- **Human assistance:** A pathway to interpreter support when automated assistance is insufficient.
- **Institutional insights:** Aggregate information to help public institutions improve accessibility.

Our goal is not to replace human interpreters or claim perfect sign-language translation. It is to reduce everyday communication barriers and make assistance easier to access.

## ♿ Accessibility Principles

Accessibility is central to SignBridge 360, not an afterthought.

We aim to incorporate:

- Clear typography and adjustable text sizes.
- High-contrast visual elements.
- Large, easy-to-use touch targets.
- Visual alternatives to audio notifications.
- Captions and readable communication.
- Keyboard-friendly navigation.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Consistent icons accompanied by meaningful labels.
- Simple, distraction-free emergency interactions.

## 🔐 Privacy & Responsible AI

We believe accessibility solutions should respect individual privacy.

Our design principles include:

- Collect only the information required for a specific interaction.
- Make sharing communication preferences voluntary.
- Avoid placing sensitive personal information directly in QR codes.
- Obtain permission before accessing cameras or microphones.
- Prioritize privacy-conscious processing of visual information.
- Clearly distinguish automated assistance from qualified human interpretation.

SignBridge 360 is an assistive communication platform, not a substitute for professional medical advice, emergency services, or qualified sign-language interpretation.
